# 50 opportunities — checked 1 October 2026

This batch is filtered for future deadlines or live ongoing programs, with an India-based undergraduate engineering student and early-stage builder as the working profile. Age, citizenship, student status, company incorporation and specialist experience remain conditions to check personally. It is not a claim that all 50 are immediately eligible for every reader.

**36 entries have a future competition/program cutoff or participation window; 14 are ongoing grants, fellowships, support programs or security rewards.** Cash, investment, credits, non-cash rewards and recognition are explicitly separated. Dates use IST where a reliable conversion is possible. A cutoff at 00:00 is the start of that day.

## How this was filtered

Used the cloud browser to select online, public, open events on Devpost; checked current Indian hackathon listings and read live organizer pages on Unstop, plus Google bounty rules. Organizer websites and current official documentation supplied the remaining verification. Earlier application stages take precedence over later event or registration dates. Search engines and aggregator sites were discovery resources rather than automatic proof of eligibility.

Removed expired or mismatched leads, including HackITon (Sept 24 registration), Open Source Sprint Hyderabad (live registration closed Sept 29), Mitacs (Sept 16), NHAI internships (final/pre-final-year restriction), MLAI (Vietnam-specific students) and Medici grants (cash restricted to North America).

## Use and maintenance

The JSON companion contains one record per opportunity, including stable ID, domain, type, deadline text, source URL, benefit, eligibility, next action, cautions, priority and verification date. Exact machine timestamps are left null rather than guessed for conflicting/date-only deadlines. The source page links onward to the actual application or submission form where necessary.

Recheck the source immediately before applying. Prioritize urgent October deadlines first, and avoid counting credits or advertised non-cash valuations as funds available to spend. Fee conflicts and full-time/dropout conditions are stated on the affected entries. No application, payment or external registration was performed.

This is a research batch for later import. The current existence or implementation of a separate database/admin panel was not inspected.
